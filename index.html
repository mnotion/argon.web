01 / PHILOSOPHY

Less friction.
More intention.

We believe good software earns its place in your day. It should feel considered, stay out of the way, and help you move from thought to action without unnecessary complexity.

Argon is an ongoing effort to make digital tools feel a little more human — clear in purpose, calm in appearance, and dependable in practice.
